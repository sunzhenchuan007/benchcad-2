# SRF geometry notes

Each sample selects one complete printed D1/D2/H1/H2/D3 row. D2 is modeled as
the retaining-flange diameter and the insertion stem fits the D3 mounting bore;
this preserves the through-panel installation interface shown by the catalogue
section. The compatible wall range S=4--12 mm is metadata, not geometry.

The radial split is cut through the full body so the bushing remains visibly
installable around a tube. The catalogue does not dimension clearance, split
width, barb depth, lead chamfer or local radii, so they are deterministic
proportions. No panel, tube or deformed installation state is emitted.
